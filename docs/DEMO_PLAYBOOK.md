# Fewer slides. A stronger live decision.

## Three-minute speaking notes

### 1 · Change the outcome — 20 seconds
“An alarm tells an operator something is wrong. It does not tell them which action is justified. NexusForge connects evidence, the next useful observation, and available recovery options—with a human making the final call.”

The factory is the app's actual procedural geometry, not a measured plant model.

### 2 · The alarm is not the explanation — 25 seconds
“Our intended user is a line supervisor under production pressure. In this synthetic case the robot has high torque, but its upstream pressure is falling. Replacing a €2,450 motor might miss an €18.50 seal. Neither diagnosis becomes true because it looks compelling.”

Prices are illustrative, not supplier quotes. Business hypothesis: sell decision support per production line; pilot reviewed diagnostic time, unnecessary interventions and verified interruption cost. No customers or validated pricing are claimed.

### 3 · One observation — 30 seconds
“The system keeps multiple explanations alive. Under explicit likelihood assumptions it calculates which observation would reduce uncertainty most. A leak-verification result changes relative support and unlocks intervention only when constraints also permit it. Scores are not calibrated failure probabilities.”

Innovation is the integrated decision experience, not inventing information gain. Model confidence never becomes authorization.

### 4 · Change the evidence — 30 seconds
“Remove the part: repair is blocked. Contradict the sensors: ask for evidence. Cross a safety threshold: escalate. For the confirmed, stocked synthetic case, modeled costs are €13,036.24 versus €1,597.06—€11,439.18 lower than an illustrative conventional baseline. Inspectable arithmetic, not realized savings.”

The baseline is a comparison, not an authorized action in the constrained pressure case. Diagnosis costs are not compared as if they restore production.

### 5 · Intelligence and accountability — 30 seconds
“The model examines hypotheses and cites evidence. AMD serves it; Evolus retrieves/extracts context and can request a workflow. Source-validated fields feed the solver. Stock, deadlines and safety policies constrain options. Human acknowledgement precedes the request. Forty-four synthetic contract checks pass.”

Mocked calls do not count as sponsor usage.” After verification, show genuine receipts/workspace usage. ### 6 · Your next move — 10 seconds
